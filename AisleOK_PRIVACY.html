AisleOK Privacy Policy

Effective 16 August 2026. Wellness app, not a medical device.

AisleOK (“the app”) is a grocery-aisle helper for people who want a simple eat / small portion / skip read on a packaged food or a produce name. This policy describes what the app does with information. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat IBS or any other condition.

Who we are

AisleOK is published by Ton Borrell Tatché. Contact the developer through the AisleOK App Store listing, or at the email published with the GitHub Pages site once the repo is live.

What the app does not do

  • No account. We do not ask you to sign up.
  • No tracking. We do not use tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, or analytics SDKs in v1.
  • No sale of personal data.
  • No upload of your scan history to our servers. v1 has no AisleOK backend.
  • We do not collect health symptoms, a food diary, or medical records.

Information the app uses

Camera. The camera is used on your device to read a grocery barcode or an ingredient label. Frames are processed on device (Apple Vision / VisionKit). We do not upload photos to AisleOK.

Barcode lookup. If you scan a barcode, the app sends that barcode (and a standard app User-Agent) to Open Food Facts, a third-party open food database, to fetch the product name and ingredient list. Open Food Facts’ terms apply to that request: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use. We do not send your name, Apple ID, or scan history with that request.

Typed search. Produce and food names you type are matched on device against AisleOK’s trigger list. They are not sent to us.

Scan history. Recent verdicts may be stored on your device so you can look them up later. You can clear them from Settings. They are not synced in v1.

Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment. We receive an on-device entitlement (whether you are subscribed), not your card number. See Apple’s privacy policy for App Store payments.

Permissions

The app requests Camera access to scan barcodes and labels. You can deny it and still use Search produce. Paid features do not require granting camera access.

Children

AisleOK is not directed at children under 13 and is not a Kids Category app.

Data retention

On-device history stays until you delete it or delete the app. Open Food Facts may retain barcode queries under their own policy. We do not keep a copy.

Your choices

  • Deny camera permission in iOS Settings.
  • Clear scan history in the app.
  • Delete the app to remove local data.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

International users

The first catalog we claim is US grocery. If you use the app from elsewhere, barcode lookup still goes to Open Food Facts (typically servers outside your country). We do not geo-lock the binary.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date on this page and the copy in the app.
