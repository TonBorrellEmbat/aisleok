What AisleOK is

AisleOK is a wellness journal-style helper. You scan a grocery barcode, photograph an ingredient label, or type a produce name. The app returns eat, small portion, skip, or unknown, and names the ingredient that drove the result when it can.

AisleOK is not a medical device, not a diagnosis, and does not treat IBS or any other condition. It does not replace a doctor or dietitian. Verdicts are conservative guesses from a small public-source trigger list plus on-pack ingredient text. Unknown is a valid result. Do not rely on the app as your only check for allergies or a medically prescribed diet.
